The first day of school always brings a mix of excitement, anticipation, and maybe a few butterflies. New classrooms, new teachers, new friends, and new routines make this time of year feel like a fresh start. A bouquet of flowers is a thoughtful way to celebrate the beginning of a new chapter, encourage a student before a big first day, or let a favorite teacher know how much they're appreciated.
